The problem

Rezoning is where good deals go to die.

Real estate companies need land, and most of it isn't zoned for what they want to build. They can pay a premium for land that's already zoned, or rezone it themselves and face government applications, public hearings, and outside consultants with no clear picture of the odds.

How it works

Everything that decides a rezoning, in one report.

Enter a parcel. Project Rezone pulls the records, rules, and local history that determine whether it gets approved, then turns them into the numbers you need to make a decision.

What it pulls in

  • Site and soil records
  • Local government fees, zoning laws, and hearing schedules
  • Local politics and community sentiment
  • Comparable rezoning cases nearby
  • Professional and filing costs

What it produces

  • Approval likelihood
  • Timeline to a decision
  • Cost range
  • Risk flags
  • Underwriting memo you can hand to lenders and partners
